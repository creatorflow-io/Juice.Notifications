| 17 | p1-05 | **Juice task: a RabbitMQ message that fails every time never stops coming back.** The intake retries through the broker (its queue dead-letters to a delay queue that returns the message), because Juice's retry tiers republish without `user_id` and the publisher would be lost (`RabbitMQIntake`). Refusals are acknowledged, so only a failure loops: a message Juice can't read (malformed JSON, or a number beyond a double such as `1e400`), a body containing the text `throw-fake-exception` (the Juice consumer engine throws on it on purpose, a test hook in production code), or a bug. Each comes back every `RetryDelay` (30 s) until removed by hand from `notifications.requests`. Juice could park after an `x-death` count, or park unreadable messages without parking failed ones. Any broker user with write on the intake exchange can do this, even without a `user_id`: Juice reads the body, and checks the marker, before the handler checks the publisher. So only give producers write on it. **Fixed 13 Nov 2026 in Juice `release/10` (6135290); the intake runs it from 13 Nov 2026 on the alpha `Juice.EventBus.RabbitMQ` 10.13.1-alpha.324:** a message that keeps failing is parked on `notifications.requests.parked` after `Intake:RabbitMQ:ParkAfter` (1 day), and one Juice can't read is parked at once; the marker is gone. A number beyond a double wasn't unreadable as said here: it was accepted as the string `"Infinity"`; it is now unreadable, and parked. | A stable Juice release: move `Juice.EventBus.RabbitMQ` off the alpha |
| 18 | p1-05 | The Juice RabbitMQ consumer doesn't retry its start: if the broker is down when the host starts, it logs and never consumes. The intake declares its topology first and fails the host's start after `Intake:RabbitMQ:StartTimeout` (60 s) instead, so an orchestrator restarts it; a broker lost later is recovered by the client. Readiness reads only the connection (`BrokerHealthCheck`): a consumer that never started, or whose channel the broker closed with a channel error (recovery doesn't restore that), still reads Healthy; Juice would need to expose the consumer's state. **Fixed 13 Nov 2026 in Juice `release/10` (6135290); the intake runs it from 13 Nov 2026 on the alpha `Juice.EventBus.RabbitMQ` 10.13.1-alpha.324:** the consumer retries its start and restarts after the broker cancels it or closes its channel, and readiness reads the consumer (`rabbitmq`, still Degraded at worst), not the connection. The intake still fails its start after `StartTimeout` when the broker can't take its topology. | A stable Juice release: move `Juice.EventBus.RabbitMQ` off the alpha |
| 19 | p1-05 review, round 1 | **Juice task: consumer prefetch has no effect.** Juice 10.13.0's consumer engine calls `BasicConsume` before `BasicQos`, and a per-consumer prefetch applies only to consumers created after it, so the broker sends the whole queue ahead unacknowledged (seen: 50 of 50). After a long outage the intake holds the whole backlog in memory, and a backlog that takes longer than the broker's `consumer_timeout` (30 min) to work through could get the channel closed. Fix in Juice: set qos before consuming. **Fixed 13 Nov 2026 in Juice `release/10` (6135290); the intake runs it from 13 Nov 2026 on the alpha `Juice.EventBus.RabbitMQ` 10.13.1-alpha.324:** `Prefetch` (10) now holds. | A stable Juice release: move `Juice.EventBus.RabbitMQ` off the alpha |
| 20 | p1-05 review, round 1 | **No inbox.** Juice's in-memory idempotency service is scoped and the dispatcher makes a scope per message, so its inbox check never sees a duplicate; duplicates are caught by the request's unique key instead (`RabbitMQIntakeTests`). A real inbox (singleton or EF) would key on the body's `MessageId`, which any producer chooses: one application could then suppress another's message. Leave it off, or key it by publisher. Also: Newtonsoft reads a number in `labels` or `links` as its text (`5` as `"5"`), which the HTTP API refuses. | With producer events (phase 3) |
| 21 | p1-05 review, round 2 | **Juice task: the consumer doesn't drain on shutdown.** Juice's consumer hosted service returns once consuming starts, and stops its channel in an unawaited callback, so the host's 45 s shutdown timeout doesn't wait for a message being handled: the container is disposed under it, and the broker redelivers it.
